MEL Paper, Greece’s largest recycling-based paper manufacturer, has adopted Trimble Wedge to accelerate its digital transformation. This process intelligence tool optimizes production data to enhance real-time decision-making, boost transparency, reduce waste, and maintain product quality. According to Mill Manager Martin Bergner, the platform demonstrated immediate value by successfully aiding the commissioning of a Combined Heat and Power (CHP) plant even before the system was fully rolled out.
