Trimble ProjectSight is a cloud-based project and site management platform designed for SME construction companies and specialist contractors. It connects the office and job site through 2D/3D BIM integration and automated drawing management, while supporting compliance. Featuring a named user licensing model, external supply chain partners (such as subcontractors) can collaborate free of charge.
